Why Fullerton
02

"Owning and operating" is not rare;
Fullerton's full combination is.

An owned brand × owned and operated in-house — the mainland already has these: Wanda Reign, Songtsam, Aranyanote.

But a public example that brings all four together at once, the way Fullerton does, is exceedingly rare.

First, lay out Fullerton's holdings (four hotels)

The brand is owned by Sino Group and run by the Sino group of companies; but common ownership of the brand does not mean all four hotels are wholly owned by Sino — a distinction worth keeping clear in any external narrative.

  • The Fullerton Hotel Singapore — 400 keys, opened 2001, a conversion of the 1928 General Post Office Building in Singapore1.
  • The Fullerton Bay Hotel Singapore — 100 keys, opened 2010, a new build on Marina Bay, its entrance linked to the conserved Clifford Pier2.
  • The Fullerton Hotel Sydney — 416 keys, opened 2019, the former Sydney GPO at No.1 Martin Place (an 1874 building)3.
  • The Fullerton Ocean Park Hotel Hong Kong — 425 keys, soft-opened 2022, the brand's first resort and first Hong Kong property; jointly developed by Sino Land and Empire Group, not wholly owned by Sino4.

Takeaway: Singapore and Sydney are flagships born of landmark heritage conversions; the Bay and Hong Kong properties extend the brand into waterfront and conservation settings.

This combination needs every part of it:

Fullerton's potential differentiation ＝ an internationally recognised heritage luxury brand ＋ in-house Sino operation ＋ a Hong Kong–Singapore narrative ＋ an owner-held city flagship.

Qianhai: Look at the Land First,
Then Talk Contract.

The first thing to establish is what that parcel actually is. Without the parcel number, red-line boundary, planning conditions, supply method, saleable-to-held ratio, and land price, any framework agreement is premature.

Policy Entry Point
The process for a Qianhai headquarters / priority-industry project is "project selection ＋ supply by tender, auction, listing-for-sale, or lease"11. The one-third tilt toward Hong Kong capital is a genuine lead, but it is an industrial-land tilt — not a private land allocation, and not the same as residential being saleable12.
The Biggest Risk
In Qianhai, office space is fully self-held — a holding period of no less than 10 years from completion, titled as a whole, with no unit subdivision and no transfer permitted13. Once this rule applies, "residential subsidising the hotel" may fail outright.

Plays B + C · Backup Lines
05

Path Design:
Two Cities ＋ Two Tracks ＋ a Brand Prelude.

The backup lines exist so that Qianhai is not the only answer. Shekou buys time; Guangzhou supplies the narrative. But neither should be dressed up as a mature target.

Shekou: Inquire, Don't Value
The Hilton Shenzhen Shekou Nanhai, 1177 Wanghai Road, is large enough at 541 keys and 4,064 sqm of meeting space14. But the legal owner, land tenure, mortgages, saleability, and management contract are all undisclosed — so this reads only "open to inquiry", not "available to acquire".
Guangzhou: Keep a Line, Don't Chase Land
Shamian's planning controls support a historical-cultural narrative: a 31.4-hectare core protection zone capped at 12 m, a 7.7-hectare construction-control belt capped at 18 m, and 54 nationally protected sites including 49 heritage buildings16. But Shamian itself is not a ready flagship site.

Why Lujulu can only serve as a template: the Bai'etan Lujulu parcel was acquired in Jan 2025 by Poly Property (the exact entity per the conveyance documents) for RMB 3.803 billion, at a floor price of around RMB 23,769/sqm, via listing-for-sale15; the conveyance terms combined commercial-residential use with a mandatory five-star hotel of no fewer than 320 keys — consistent with the supply logic of "a self-held flagship hotel ＋ residential", and useful as a reference (one case, not yet enough to extrapolate into general policy).

Its value, then, is not in whether we can get it but in being a ready template: the land is already Poly's and is not available. Shamian works as a narrative anchor but cannot carry a flagship.

Shekou asks can the flag be switched; Guangzhou asks where the next parcel is. Those two questions are enough — don't draw the market's conclusions for it.

How the Numbers Work · This Is Not a Feasibility Study
06

The Profit Mechanism:
"residential subsidising the hotel" is still an assumption.

The logic of this play is to use the proceeds of saleable residential to cover the heavy-asset cost of the self-held hotel. But it remains an unverified assumption — the margin, the hotel's net cost, and the cash-flow timing have yet to be worked through.

The Hongrongyuan Center (Qianhai Guiwan, a super-Grade-A complex of office ＋ Conrad ＋ retail ＋ sea-view apartments) can serve as a benchmark for "saleable uses coexisting with a self-held hotel" (see 04)25; but its margin, the hotel's net cost, and cash-flow timing are all undisclosed, so "residential subsidising the hotel" remains an inference and cannot yet show that the path is bound to work. indirect source (benchmark)

Ultra-prime absorption confirms the purchasing power is here — Shenzhen Bay's Yunxi sold RMB 13 billion on its launch day and RMB 23.9 billion over four months on the market; Qianhai's China Merchants Haiyan Mansion was filed at RMB 142,000/sqm and sold about 90% on launch day9. indirect source (media-verified) But this proves only that core, scarce residential has purchasing power; it does not prove that any hotel ＋ residential complex can recover its investment from residential — a full set of modelling sits in between.

For "residential subsidy" to hold, five preconditions must all be met
  1. the parcel permits a large enough volume of saleable residential;
  2. the saleable residential can be sold by subdivision and generate proceeds;
  3. after land, taxes, financing, marketing, and the build period, a net cash contribution remains;
  4. the self-held hotel is not dragged down by the industry oversight agreement or the REITs / asset structure;
  5. the cash-flow timing of residential sales and hotel construction can be matched.

If even one fails, the whole logic struggles to hold — so the figures below are only an order-of-magnitude framework, meant to gauge scale, not to ground a decision.

REITs exit: current state and limits

At the end of 2025, NDRC Investment Circular [2025] No. 991 brought four-star-and-above hotels into the asset pool eligible for REITs17 — turning securitisation from essentially unfeasible into an option. But for mixed projects the hotel plus commercial/office area is capped, and the original equity holder may not engage in commodity residential development, so residential and the held hotel must be structurally separated, and this cannot be used to back-justify the land-acquisition model.

备用线的价值，是让前海不变成唯一答案。蛇口可以换时间；广州可以换叙事。但两个都不能被包装成已经成熟的标的。

蛇口：探询，不估值
深圳蛇口希尔顿南海酒店，望海路 1177 号，541 间客房、4,064㎡ 会议空间，体量足够14。但法律业主、土地年限、抵押、可出售性、管理合同都没有公开——只写「可探询」，不写「可收购」。
广州：留线，不追地
沙面控规支持历史文化叙事：核心保护范围 31.4 公顷限高 12 米，建设控制地带 7.7 公顷限高 18 米，54 处国保含 49 栋文物建筑16。但沙面本身不是现成旗舰地块。

陆居路为什么只能当模板：白鹅潭陆居路地块 2025-01 由保利置业（主体名以出让文件为准）以 38.03 亿元取得、楼面价约 23,769 元/㎡，挂牌出让15；出让条件是商住综合 ＋ 强制配建五星酒店不少于 320 间——这与「自持旗舰酒店 ＋ 住宅」的供地逻辑一致，可作一处参照（单一案例，尚不足以外推为普遍政策）。

所以它的价值不在「能不能拿到」，而在于它是个现成模板：地已被保利拿走，非可拿地块。沙面适合当叙事锚，承载不了旗舰。

蛇口问「能不能换牌」，广州问「下一块在哪里」。这两句就够，不要替市场下结论。

利润机制：
「住宅反哺酒店」目前仍是假设。

这套打法的逻辑是：用可售住宅的回款，覆盖自持酒店的重资产成本。但它目前仍是待验证假设——利润率、酒店净成本、现金流时序都还没被打开。

鸿荣源中心（前海桂湾，超甲级综合体，写字楼 ＋ 康莱德酒店 ＋ 商业 ＋ 海景公寓）可作为「可售业态与自持酒店并存」的对标案例（详见 04）25；不过它的利润率、酒店净成本、现金流时序均未公开，「住宅反哺酒店」更多是推断，还推不出「这条路必然跑通」。间接来源（对标）

顶豪去化能说明这里有购买力——深圳湾澐玺首开单日销 130 亿、入市四个月累计 239 亿；前海招商海晏府备案价 14.2 万/㎡、开盘当天去化约九成9。间接来源（媒体已核） 不过这只能证明「核心稀缺住宅有购买力」，还不能证明任何「酒店 ＋ 住宅综合体」都能靠住宅覆盖总投——两者之间隔着一整套测算。

「住宅反哺」要成立，需同时满足五个前提
  1. 地块允许足够规模的可售住宅；
  2. 可售住宅能分割销售并回款；
  3. 扣除土地、税费、融资、营销、建设周期后仍有净现金贡献；
  4. 自持酒店不被产业监管协议或 REITs / 资产结构反向拖累；
  5. 住宅销售与酒店建设的现金流时序能匹配。

只要一条不成立，整套逻辑就难以为继——所以下面的数字都只是数量级框架，用来把握量级，不作决策依据。
