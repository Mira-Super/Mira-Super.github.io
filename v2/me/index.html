01 · Me

ME

Most ESG practitioners study industries from the outside. I spent fifteen years in rooms where decisions are made — civil aviation, real estate, defense-civil integration, new energy, investment — before sustainability became a mandate. I didn't pivot to ESG. I chose it, because the industries I know are going to transition whether they're ready or not. I'd rather be on the right side of that.
